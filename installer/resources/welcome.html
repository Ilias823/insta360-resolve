Insta360 Stitch for DaVinci Resolve

Edit Insta360 X5 360° footage directly in DaVinci Resolve: stitch it, reframe it into normal or vertical video, and follow subjects automatically.

Before you continue, quit DaVinci Resolve.

You need:

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio 21.1 or later

Nothing else is needed: no Homebrew, Python or ffmpeg.
