Installed

To bring in footage: open a project in DaVinci Resolve, choose Workspace > Scripts > Insta360 Import and pick your .insv clips. They appear in the Insta360 bin as <name> 360.

To edit: put a clip on a timeline of any shape, then drag Insta360 Stitch (Effects > Open FX > Insta360) onto it. Choose a View Style, or drag in the viewer to aim.

For the on-screen controls (dragging, zoom bar, tracking box), set the viewer's menu at its bottom-left to Open FX Overlay.

The lens files are written to an insta360_ingest folder next to each clip; they take about as much space as the clip.

Insta360 Stitch is free and open source. If it saves you time, you can support its development on Ko-fi. Thank you!

To remove everything later, run /Library/Application Support/Insta360Stitch/uninstall.sh in Terminal.
